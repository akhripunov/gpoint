# GPoint Core Product V1

GPoint keeps free creator content, discovery, and acquisition inside the Mini App. VIP is an outbound link to the creator's approved Telegram seller flow. GPoint does not collect Stars, split creator revenue, claim subscriptions, or show affiliate earnings in this phase.

The implemented product surface is a visually testable fixture experience: Explore tabs, creator profiles, free media tiles, Reels, VIP shortlist, Profile, creator onboarding/dashboard, and a compact moderation queue. Fixture creators are fictional and contain no revenue claims.
